## Summary

Nothing is broken. Both are rare by design of the current numbers.

How they appear in the current code (`src/systems/events.ts`):
- One random event every 70-140 s of game time (average 105 s), from hole level 2. Four-leaf Clover divides the wait by 1 + 0.15 per level.
- The event is drawn by weight from the rows whose gate is true. Day pool at level 4-9: 11 events, total weight 20.5. At night from level 6 the Night Visitor (weight 3) joins, total 23.5. At level 10+ the King (0.6) joins.
- UFO: weight 1.5, from hole level 4. Share 1.5 / 20.5 = 7.3 % by day, 6.4 % at night. Nothing else blocks it; `spawnUfo` only skips when a UFO is already flying (it leaves after 45 s).
- King: weight 0.6, from hole level 10, and only while no King is alive. Factory and rain never make him (`weight: 0`, `unlock: 99` in `src/data/gnomeTypes.ts`). One guaranteed King 6 s after reaching level 10 (`src/systems/hole.ts:252`). Below level 10 he never comes. After a rebirth the hole is back at level 1, so no King until level 10 again.
- Later features only diluted the pool a little: Gnomenado added 1.5 weight (UFO share 7.9 % → 7.3 %), the Night Visitor row adds 3 at night. No feature filters out the UFO or the King.

Expected time between (no Clover), computed: UFO about 24 min by day, 27 min at night; King about 62 min at level 10+ (21.1 / 0.6 = 35 events x 105 s).

Measured headless (real `Events.update`, `start` stubbed to count, 200 runs x 20 game minutes per case):

| case | min per event | min per UFO | min per King | median time to first UFO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| level 6 day | 1.76 | 24.7 | never (gate) | 16.9 min |
| level 6 night | 1.76 | 32.3 | never (gate) | over 20 min |
| level 10 day | 1.75 | 24.5 | 75.5 | 16.1 min |
| level 10 night | 1.76 | 27.2 | 74.1 | 16.9 min |
| level 10 day, Clover 3 | 1.21 | 19.7 | 47.6 | 13.9 min |

Real game loop at level 10: a started UFO flies in (`events.ufo` set, mesh in the scene, y = 14); a started King spawns (1 King in `gnomes.list`); 12 forced timer picks gave `ufo, burp, gnomenado, quake, rush, wind, bloom, quake, burp, golden, rain, golden` (the live pool includes the UFO).

So: in a normal 20-30 minute session there is about a 50 % chance of no UFO at all, and the King comes about once an hour at level 10+, never below 10.

## Question for PP (balance, not changed)

How often should the UFO and the King come?
- a) (recommended) Own timers outside the random pool: UFO every 4-6 min from hole level 4; King once per hole level from 10 (2-4 min after the level-up), plus about every 15 min while no King is alive. Then PP sees both reliably, and other events keep their share.
- b) Only raise weights: UFO 1.5 → 5 (about every 9 min), King 0.6 → 3 (about every 15 min at level 10+). Simple, but every other event comes less often.
- c) Leave as is (UFO about every 25 min, King about every hour from level 10).
