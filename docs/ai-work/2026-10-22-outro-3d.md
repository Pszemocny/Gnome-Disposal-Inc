Deviations from the plan:
- Scenario file is `tools/smoke/scenario-outro-3d.js` (name from the order); Low, daily and the pile variants run in iframes.
- Kings stand in the 5th row (y ≈ 4.6), not y ≈ 3: at y 3 they are outside the upward view.
- Far eyes are a second `Points` with size 0.2 and pair spacing 0.3 m, over the whole wall (y 1 to 38) and dimmed with distance (coordinator's visual note 1).
- A directional light from the top of the shaft, built once at `start()` with the scene (coordinator's visual note 2); the yard scene is untouched, so the no-runtime-lights rule holds.
- Shaft wall and floor merged into one mesh, so the letters mesh fits the 20-call budget.
- Fallers live in the spinning drum with the walls.
- The pile is a mound, not shrinking rings: the plan's layout made a tower of up to 30 m at the caps.
- Lights and fog dimmer than the plan.
- The HUD hides while `.deep` is in the DOM (`body:has(.deep)`).
- `.deep-fade` uses `z-index: -1` inside `.deep`: `.deep-lines` is `display: contents`.
- Done-when 20 asserts at most one render per frame and at least one.
- `IGNORED` in `intro.ts` is no longer exported (no skip, no second user).
