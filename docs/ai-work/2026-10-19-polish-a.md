# Polish round, Package A: impact and gnome feel (2026-10-19)

Plan: `docs/ai-work/2026-10-19-plan-polish-round.md`, section "Package A". Files touched: `src/entities/gnomes.ts`, `src/core/audio.ts`, `src/systems/fx.ts` only.

## Problem

Thrown and dropped gnomes landed without any feedback, a gnome on the magnet looked like a dead weight, and statues woke up without a visible moment.

## Summary

- Landing impact: a ragdoll gnome whose vertical speed jumps up by more than 5 m/s since the last frame (ground or a machine stopped its fall) gets `fx.dust(pos, strength)` (6-12 brown puffs spreading sideways, counted in `fx.stats.dust`), `audio.impact(strength, mass)` (low sine plus short noise, lower for heavier bodies) and a 0.15 s squash (y x0.7, xz x1.2, smoothstep back) applied in `writeInstances`. New gnome fields: `prevVy`, `squash`, `screamed`.
- Struggle on the magnet: a held gnome wiggles around its own up axis (8 Hz, ±0.25 rad, render only) and says one of four struggle lines every 1.5-3 s through `say()`. The countdown reuses `g.timer`, which the held state did not use; `hold()` arms it, `drop()` resets it as before.
- Throw scream: a gnome thrown by the player (`throwPos` set) that flies faster than 10 m/s plays `audio.voice('scream')` once, pitch `(1.2 / mass)^0.35` (0.5 kg ≈ 1.36, 3 kg ≈ 0.73).
- Statue wake-up: `wake()` gives a statue an upward impulse of 3 x mass and a light puff. All `wake()` callers (gnome update loop, events parade and uprising) run outside Rapier query callbacks.

Drabina: shelf 2 for everything possible (existing `puffs` particle pool, `say()`, `tone()`/`noise()`, `tmpQ` style temporaries); new code only where nothing existed (`dust`, `impact`, the landing check).

Deviation: the plan says the scream flag resets "on landing". It resets on each new throw instead (in `drop()` with an impulse). Reason: a gnome thrown at 15 m/s keeps more than 10 m/s of horizontal speed after the first bounce, so a reset on landing would scream twice for one throw, which breaks the Done-when "exactly once for that throw".

Odrzucone: impact on statues (state `statue`) dropped from height: the plan limits it to `ragdoll`, and statues wake into ragdoll anyway.
