## Problem

PP after playtest: the game feels weak. Package B makes eating at the hole feel bigger: rim pulse, faster/tinted swirl, coin fountain that lands outside the hole, coins sliding to the player, level-up shockwave, combo tier ray burst.

## Summary

- `environment.ts`: the rim mesh is kept as `env.rim`. New `env.holePulse(strength)` makes the rim pulse 1 → 1 + 0.12·strength → 1 over 0.25 s and spins the swirl faster for 1 s. From combo 10 the swirl material colour tints white (the texture's own blue) → purple → gold by combo / 60.
- `hole.ts`: `consume` calls `holePulse` when the gnome fell into the hole (not for blackhole machines; strength 1.6 for massive or bullseye). Level up adds a ground shockwave ring (0 → 12 m in 0.8 s, fading). It also collects free gnomes within 10 m through `gnomes.forEachNear`, then ragdolls them and pushes them away and up. The push happens after collecting, outside any Rapier query. Each combo tier shows a star of 10 coloured glow sprites above the hole for 0.6 s, with no lights.
- `coins.ts`: coins fly in a fountain, spread evenly around 360°, upward 9-13 m/s and outward 2-5 m/s (scaled by √power). For hole coins the outward speed is raised so the predicted landing point is at least holeRadius + 1.3 from the centre. In flight, a young coin (< 3 s) that comes down over the hole gets an outward kick. An older coin that rolled onto the hidden ground under the vortex is nudged in and refunded by the existing `y < -5` branch. Coins within 6 m of the player steer towards them, faster when closer: the code steers to a target speed, because plain acceleration lost to ground friction. Coins collected within 0.35 s of each other chime a semitone higher each time, up to an octave.
- Ladder: everything reuses existing pieces: `glowTexture`, `forEachNear`, `ragdoll`, `audio.coin(pitch)`, and the existing refund branch. Only the minimum new code.

Rejected:
- `pickupRadius` (upgrade `magnetRange`) unchanged: the 6 m pull is a slide, the instant pickup still uses the upgrade, so its balance stays the same.
- Coins keep rolling on their edge for several seconds (speeds 2-4 m/s after 6 s in the test). This was there before (random spin from the existing code), it is not in package B → a separate task if PP wants it.
