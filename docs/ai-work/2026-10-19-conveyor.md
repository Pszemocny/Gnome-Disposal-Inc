# Conveyor belt that really carries gnomes (2026-10-19)

Plan: `2026-10-19-plan-conveyor.md`, section "Plan (plan-senior, final)". Only the conveyor parts of `src/entities/machines.ts` changed (another agent works on the crate in the same file).

## Problem

PP: belts "do not work at all"; wants a nice animation, real carrying along the whole length, a toss at the end and side walls.

## Cause (reproduced before the change)

Scenario `conv-repro.js` (old code): a gnome dropped on local z = -1.3 moved to z = +2.75 in about 1.3 s, then stood up and walked back. So the push itself worked, but:

- The belt showed the opposite direction. On the BoxGeometry top face the texture `v` grows towards local -Z (measured: dz/dv = -2.94). The old chevron tip was at the top of the canvas (high `v`, so -Z) and the old scroll (`offset.y -= ...`) moved the pattern towards -Z. The push goes to +Z. When a player loads the belt at the end the arrows start from, the gnome lands on the exit, gets 0.3 m of push and falls off. (Inference: this is what PP saw as "does not work at all".)
- All belts shared one texture, so with N belts the scroll ran N times faster.
- Belt friction 1.2 against the forced speed made gnomes tumble: the speed dropped to 0.9 m/s mid-belt.
- No side walls, no toss, rollers were merged into one static mesh.

## Summary of changes (`src/entities/machines.ts`)

- Visual: low base, two side rails 0.35 m above the belt (open ends), yellow cone arrows on the rails pointing to +Z, three separate rollers with a yellow stripe that spin with the belt, belt with its own texture copy. Chevron redrawn with the tip at the bottom of the canvas (+Z).
- Colliders: base top at 0.35 with friction 0.3, two rail colliders (0.05 x 0.24 x 1.6 half extents at x = +-0.7), same group as the base.
- Effect: zone between the rails, along the belt, 0.1..1.65 above the origin (surface 0.35). Speed 4.5 m/s along local +Z, centring -2 * x, the normal speed only kept when it points into the belt (works on ramps). Gnomes stay ragdoll (timer >= 0.8). Last 0.3 m: 6 m/s forward + 3 m/s along the belt's up, timer >= 1.5, `thrownBy = 'machine'` unless it is a player throw with `throwPos` (trick flags untouched). Texture offset grows at speed * repeat / length (pattern moves at 4.5 m/s towards +Z), rollers spin at speed / radius.
- `MACHINE_BOX.conveyor` = half [0.75, 0.43, 1.6], centre [0, 0.43, 0] (rail arrows reach 0.86).

Ladder: everything reuses the existing conveyor code, `build`, `vcMat`, `stripeTexture`, `this.local/up/forward`; no new helpers.
