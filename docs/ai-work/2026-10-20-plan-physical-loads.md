# Plan: physical loads (gnomes stay visible inside the crate and on the robot bed)

Spec: PP 2026-10-20 (verbatim): "Sprobuj poprawic animacje ladowania do robotow skrzynki chodzi o to aby te gnomy nie znikaly tylko po prostu byly w skrzynce fizyka scian itd tak samo z robotami, moga byc wieksze moze sie uda". SPEC.md lines 97 (crate), 127-131 (robots), 31 (crate dump), 240 (save keeps no cargo).
Trivial check: three entity files plus a model file, a new gnome state read by every `isFree` user (robots, magnets, fans, tornado, events, player), physics bodies change type, replay buffer and the crate/robot economy paths are touched.

## Plan v0 (plan-author)

### Goal
A gnome caught by a crate or scooped by a robot keeps its body and its picture: it lands inside the container against real walls, settles, then rides with the container as a rigid load (kinematic), and comes out again as a real body when the crate tips over or the robot tips its bed; nothing is hidden except in the cannon.

### Design in one paragraph (the hybrid the brief asked to consider)
Full dynamics for the load is rejected with numbers (Alternatives). Chosen: a gnome that enters a container is **loading** (state stays `ragdoll`, `g.container = m`, not free): dynamic body, normal collision groups, so it bounces off the crate floor and walls (the crate has dominance 1, `machines.ts:384`, so it cannot be pushed by the gnome). When its speed drops below `SETTLE_SPEED = 0.3` m/s, or after `SETTLE_T = 1.2` s, it is **frozen**: `body.setBodyType(KinematicPositionBased)`, state `loaded`, collision groups `LOADED = groups(G.GNOME, G.GROUND | G.GNOME | G.PLAYER | G.STATIC)` (no `G.PROP`: a kinematic body overlapping the crate's own floor would otherwise push the dynamic crate), and `g.local` = its pose in the container's cargo frame. Every frame the container writes `setNextKinematicTranslation/Rotation` for its frozen load from its own body pose (only when the container moved since the last write), and `writeInstances` draws a `loaded` gnome from the container's **drawn** pose × `g.local` (exact picture, physics at most one step behind). On tip-over, bed tip, X pick-up: `release` turns the body dynamic again at its current pose with the container's velocity, which is the real pour. Capacity stays a counter (`m.queue.length` vs `crateCap()` / `robotCap()`), with the container sized so the counter can physically fit (Open decision 1).

### Data
- No database. Rapier world: ≤ 320 gnome bodies (`maxGnomes` 120 + 5 × 40), ≤ 6 crates × 5 colliders, ≤ 12 robots × (1 truck + 4 bed colliders). Loaded max: 6 × 60 = 360 crate slots, 12 × 17 = 204 robot slots, both above the 320 gnome cap, so the worst case is 320 kinematic bodies.
- Per-frame cost added: cargo writes only for containers that moved: ≤ 320 × (one `Matrix4` compose + `setNextKinematicTranslation` + `setNextKinematicRotation`), i.e. ≤ 640 WASM calls ≈ 0.1-0.3 ms; settling loop ≤ queued-and-loading gnomes (a handful); kinematic-kinematic pairs are never generated by Rapier, kinematic-vs-dynamic contacts only when a free gnome or the player touches a heap.

### Assumptions (numbered)
1. Loading gnomes keep state `ragdoll` with `g.container` set; only frozen ones are `loaded`. One `isFree` change covers every consumer.
2. The crate's dominance group 1 (PP 2026-10-19 "gnomy nie przesuwają skrzyni") is what lets dynamic loading gnomes hit the walls without moving the crate; the robot truck has the same dominance and mass 150.
3. Kinematic load has no weight for the magnet spring (`pull` applies `mass × a` to the crate body only, `player.ts:673-690`), so "the crate always lifts" holds without touching the spring.
4. A gnome that bounces out during the settle is simply free again (no retry, no counter fix); the robot re-targets it on its next scan because `skip` is not touched.
5. Released gnomes under an upside-down crate stay there until the player lifts the crate (rare: a kick flip); not handled.
6. The physics pose of a frozen load may be one step (≤ 8 cm at 4.9 m/s) behind the container; only free gnomes touching a heap can notice.
7. The replay shows the load moving while its truck stays at the live pose (machines are not recorded, SPEC 235); accepted as today's crate/robot replay limit, not fixed here.
8. Containers grow with their upgrade level and are rebuilt in place on purchase (row 11, 24); no re-placing by the player.
9. Robot throughput drops: a pick now takes `PICK_TIME` + settle (≈ 0.35 + 0.5-1.2 s) instead of 0.35 s; the floor of 150 eaten per 60 s with 12 robots is the regression guard.
10. No decision in `docs/ai-work` older than 60 days is used (all cited notes are 2026-10-19/20).

- (c) Disabled bodies drawn at offsets (picture only): rejected because PP asked for wall physics; a new gnome would fall through the drawn heap and overlap it.
- (d) Scale the whole truck 1.3×: rejected because the truck width drives `CLEAR` (0.7) and the avoid tuning (gaps < 1.4 m closed); a bigger bed on the same cab keeps the A* grid untouched.

- (f) Robot bed as a separate kinematic body hinged by a revolute joint: rejected; the bed colliders stay flat on the truck body, the pour gets its forward velocity at release, and the picture rotates with `bed.rotation.x` as today.
