## Summary

- `state.factoryAuto` (default true, not kept through a rebirth; old saves get true from the spread in `loadState`).
- Lever in `factory.ts`: post + housing, an arm on a pivot, a glow-sprite lamp (green ON, red OFF), one fixed collider; `toggle()` flips it with an anticipation (0.08 s, `outCubic`) and an `outBack` swing (0.27 s), a burst and a sound. `autoRate` is 0 when off; E on the lever never cranks. Offline payout is skipped when off. HUD label `GNOME FACTORY · AUTO OFF`.
- `fuses` upgrade (Tools, $300 x1.8, max 5, hole 5, not placeable; Keep Tools carries it). `breakMachine` blows a fuse while any is green; `repair` restores and swaps one fuse. Fuses and boxes are two `InstancedMesh` objects (+2 draw calls), rebuilt from `blown` + `fuseAnim` every frame (`fusePose`, `fuseColor`).
- E order in normal play: lever → broken tool → desk → tool with red fuses. In placing mode: lever → tool that needs repair.
- Texts: legend E row and saboteur row, menu how-to line, Auto Crank desc, shop EFFECT line, `docs/PLAN.md`.

Ladder: `InstancedMesh` (repo pattern, rung 2), `glowTexture`, `ease.ts`, `fx.burst`, `audio.buy/thud` (rung 2); offline guard, HUD label, legend, menu, desc, EFFECT (rung 5); new code only for the lever, the fuse state + draw and the two curves (rung 6).

Accepted, no code (plan R10, R12):
- When a tool was saved on the lever spot (factory + 2.6 m to the right), it loads inside the lever post (two fixed bodies, no crash); a crate or robot parked there is pushed out once. New placements see the collider.
- When the player holds E to crank and the crosshair passes over the lever, cranking pauses for those frames. Without that guard an E press on the lever would also crank for one frame.
- Every new tool (reload, X, G, blueprint LOAD, Z undo) starts with green fuses, like `broken 0` today (plan A6).

Deviations from the plan:
1. The lever arm swings sideways (`pivot.rotation.z`), not `rotation.x`. From the player's spot in front of the lever an x swing goes toward and away from the camera, and ON and OFF looked the same in the screenshot.
2. `leverAngle` starts at the saved state (-0.6 ON / 0.6 OFF), not 0, so the arm rests where the lamp says.
3. Lever geometry lives in `models.ts` (`leverBaseGeometry`, `leverArmGeometry`): `box`/`cyl` are private there. Knob yellow, not red, so it is not mistaken for the red lamp; lamp at the housing top behind the arm.
4. Robot mount `[0, 0.45, -0.92, π]` instead of `[0, 0.72, -0.85, π]` (y -0.27, z -0.07, inside the 0.3 m allowance): at 0.72 the box floated above the truck, at -0.75 it sat inside the glass dome.
5. Fuses stand at z 0.05 instead of 0.02: at 0.02 they sat inside the 0.08 m deep box and could not be seen.
6. The fuse base uses the tool's own scale (`m.root.scale`) instead of 1, so the box follows the placement pop.
7. `drawFuses` skips a tool when the instance caps (170 / 34) would overflow; today's max values never reach them.
8. Test setup: Done-when 9 and 21 run in the page (`new Offline().boot()`, `saveState` / `loadState` / `machines.restore`) instead of an iframe; 22 uses an iframe because rebirth reloads the page. Done-when 30 starts at `blown 3` (not 2) so the plan's own index `fuseAnim.i === 2` holds. Done-when 24 now uses a fan aimed at the sky; see "Follow-up: step 24 flaky in full runs" below for the cause and the fan fix.

Cause, as a fact about current code (traced per second in a full run, `sabdbg6`): after the first blow the saboteur wandered in front of the fan, walked back into its stream and got tripped. The fan trips every walking gnome inside its 10 m cone (`machines.ts`, case `fan`), also at the far end where the push is almost zero. There the ragdoll slides to about 9.9 m and stops; it stands up after 2 s, and on that same frame the fan trips it again with a fresh 2 s timer. It never walks again, so it never reaches the fan: one blow, then nothing for 40 s. Random events were ruled out (`events.next` 59.9 → 34.7 during the step, no event running). Solo runs pass because the saboteur usually stays behind the fan.

Ladder: rung 6, one guard on an existing line plus one constant; no helper for this exists.

Fix (game, `src/entities/machines.ts`): new `FAN_TRIP = 9`. The fan knocks over walking and sleeping gnomes only within 9 m. Between 9 and 10 m it no longer touches them; airborne and rolling gnomes still get the push up to 10 m. A tripped gnome rolls out to about 9.5-9.9 m, stands up there and walks again.

Fixture (scenario, step 24): the fan is aimed at the sky and the saboteur spawns at z 10. Reason: with an upright fan the saboteur's random wander sometimes ends in front of it, and then it can never reach the fan (see the open question below), so the step measured the wander, not the fuses.

Open question for PP: when a saboteur ends up in front of a fan, it walks into the stream, falls over, rolls back, stands up and tries again, for ever. It never sabotages that fan and never picks another tool (it always targets the nearest one). This was already true before the fix; before it, the saboteur stayed lying on the grass for ever instead. Options: a) leave it: a fan protects itself from the front; b) a saboteur that the fan knocked over goes for another tool or walks round to the back. Recommendation: a), no code.
