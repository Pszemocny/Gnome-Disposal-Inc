- GPU (estimate, RGBA + mipmaps): grass 1024² 5.6 MB; 512² maps: dirt x2 (tube 4x1, worn ground 1x1), wood x2 (posts/crate, rails; +1 per land tier), plaster, roof, metal = 7 x 1.4 = 9.8 MB; mottle 256² 0.35 MB; swirl 0.35 MB; wear alpha 64² ≈ 0. Total ≈ 16 MB (before ≈ 39 MB, plus 0.35 MB more after every hole level, see swirl below).
- Code:
  - `environment.ts`: ground `map: tex('grass_diff', 1 / 6)` without the normal map; seeded obstacles removed (`rebuildObstacles`, its meshes, fixed colliders, materials and the `seeded()` RNG); trees and bushes `vcMat({ map: tex('mottle') })`; one shared swirl texture (`swirlTex ??= makeSwirlTexture()`); worn ground (`wornGround(r)`, package 5).
  - `machines.ts`: crate `vcMat({ map: tex('wood_diff') })`, emissive hack removed.
  - `factory.ts`: body `map: tex('metal_diff')` (plates lifted to light grey so purple stays purple; the old comment said the photo metal turned it black).
  - `props.ts`: prop bush `vcMat({ map: tex('mottle') })`.
  - `textures.ts`: comment no longer names Poly Haven. `docs/PLAN.md` line 8: CC0 textures allowed.
- Ladder: asset files rung 6 (asked for); `tex()`/`vcMat({ map })` reused (rung 2); `mergeGeometries`, `PlaneGeometry`, `CanvasTexture` reused for the worn ground (rung 2/3); tone changes with the already installed `ffmpeg` (rung 4), resizing with `sips` (rung 3).
- Tone changes, decided on screenshots: the grass was dark (blurred 0.8 and gamma 1.45, now a bright lawn); the dirt was dark red-brown and the worn ground looked like burnt stains (gamma 1.7, saturation 0.8, alpha centre 0.6); metal and mottle lifted so they lighten, not darken, the vertex colours. Grass tint pair 0xc8f0a0 → 0xe8d890 kept, the drying still shows (`12-dry.png`).
- Worn ground: 16 soft patches 2-3.5 m in a ring outside the hole (each patch's soft edge ends at size/2, its centre stays at least that far outside the radius) plus up to 8 patches from the factory's landing spot to the hole, stopping before they would reach over it. One merged mesh in `holeGroup`, one shared material (dirt map + 64² radial alpha), old geometry disposed on rebuild. +1 draw call, ≤ 48 triangles, +2 small textures.
- Swirl texture: before, every `rebuildHole()` made a new 256² canvas texture that stayed on the GPU. Measured on the baseline: 3 rebuilds with the hole in view → `memory.textures` 14 → 17. Now 17 → 17. Plan line 6 and the behaviour row "hole level up x3: memory.textures unchanged" require it.

- "rocks, hole lvl ≥ 2: mottled grey" → no rocks at any level.
- The hole still moves 1.2 m per level (`holeCenterFor` untouched).

Rejected:

- The prop bush by the shovel (`props.ts`) stays fixed and without a collider: it is not random and nothing collides with it, so PP's "cannot be moved" complaint does not apply; it hides the shovel secret.

- Trees: the mottle is faint at tree distance (cone UVs spread the 256² map over a whole cone); kept at repeat 1, a stronger grain would need a darker mottle and makes the forest dirty.
