## Plan v0 (plan-author)

### Goal
Every cleaning robot has a small station (pad, pole with the fuse box, lamp, area ring) at its post; the fuse box lives there, E/G/X work when the player aims at the pole or at the truck, and the area ring is drawn at the new post after every move.

A saboteur already walks to `m.pos` (the post) and hits when within 1.8 m of it (`gnomes.ts:714-726` → `nearestWorking` `machines.ts:568`, `breakMachine` :582). Only the fuse box picture is on the truck (`FUSE_AT.robot`, `machines.ts:65`, `fuseBase` :633 uses `m.root`). So "saboteurs must walk to the station" is a drawing change, not an AI change.

### Assumptions (numbered)
1. `m.pos` keeps meaning "where the truck parks" and is the station centre; the pole stands 1.2 m behind it in the placement direction. The truck (0.9 m long from its centre, arrival within 0.6 m) can overlap the pole picture in rare poses; the pole has no collider for the truck, so nothing jams.
2. `castRay`, `castRayAndGetNormal` and `intersectionsWithShape` with default query groups hit a collider whose filter is `G.PLAYER` only (Rapier tests `(a.member & b.filter) && (b.member & a.filter)` with the query's groups = all).
3. `initRobot` is the only builder of the marker/station, so every move path (G, X + place, restore, blueprint) goes through it; nothing else creates robots (`rebuild` is called only for `conveyor`).
4. The saboteur AI is untouched: it already targets `m.pos` (station), row C3 only proves it.
5. The shop badge hides the station (step 5): the 60 px thumbnail would otherwise show a 2.6 m pad with a tiny truck.
6. The area ring bug may not reproduce on this tree (the 5300 build is older); step 1 runs red first and records the probe line either way.
7. Decisions this plan relies on are all from 2026-10-20/21 (fuses, robots-more, physical-loads, auto-lands, tree-expansion): none older than 60 days.

### Alternatives rejected
- (a) Truck parks 1.4 m in front of the pole (dock) with `m.pos` = pole: rejected, `home` is read in six places of `robots.ts` (return, idle check, toPost, step-out, area centre, target drop) and the saved `pos` would change meaning for old saves.
- (b) Station as a sensor collider: rejected, the player would walk through the pole as through today's stick; member `STATIC` with filter `PLAYER` gives a solid pole for the player and a ghost pole for trucks and gnomes in one argument.
- (c) Fuse box on both the truck and the station: rejected, doubles the instance counts and the saboteur hits only one place.
- (d) G at the station makes the truck drive to the new station with its cargo: rejected, G is "pick up and re-place" for every tool and the cargo path (`remove` → `release`) already exists; offered as Open decision 2 b).
- (e) Pole solid for everyone: rejected, a truck arriving from the far side can end within 0.3 m of the pole and would push against a fixed body (stuck/back-off loop).
