# Research: the game that inspired ours (moved from docs/RESEARCH.md)

Moved here on 2026-10-20 when PP renamed the game to Gnome Disposal Inc.: the public docs no longer name the other game, the research stays in the decision log as written.

## Facts (from public pages, 2026-10-19)

- Title: **GnomeBoozled**, developer/publisher **PyroPhysics**. Steam App 4794780, demo App 5124070 (demo released 2026-09-21, full release planned Q1 2027). Also on itch.io with a WebGL build that players report as heavy and laggy in Chrome.
- Genre: first-person physics sandbox + incremental (idle-ish) progression. Tags: Automation, Incremental, Physics, Psychological Horror, Sandbox.
- Premise: you run an illegal gnome disposal business in your backyard. You feed garden gnomes into a mysterious hole for money.
- The hole: "the more gnomes you feed it, the bigger and hungrier it becomes". It has levels (progress bar with `x2 → $2`, i.e. a value multiplier per level) and "hides secrets deep down".
- Gnomes: statues that come alive. Awakened gnomes curse, spit, sabotage machinery, steal profits. Types differ (greedier, angrier, own goals). Named in patch notes: dwarf gnome. Screenshots show a wizard gnome (purple robe, staff) and a dark shadow gnome with glowing eyes (horror element).
- Gnome houses (mushroom-shaped cottages) spawn gnomes. A fix mentions houses spawning after the hole level is maxed.
- Coins fly out of the hole in several colors (bronze, silver, gold, blue = denominations).
- Enchanted mushrooms drop from gnomes: boost combo potential, movement speed, or "more chaos".
- Combo system with escalating audiovisual feedback.
- Shop on an old PC in the house garage. Tabs: **Movement, Tools, Products, Combos, Bonuses**.
- Tools tab (from screenshot): Wooden Box (lvl x/4, $15), Cooling Fan (x/8, $500), Metal Panel (x/20, $750, "+1 metal panel, place on any static surface, create covers, tunnels, ramps to guide gnomes into hole"), Kicking Wheel (x/1, $8000), Magnet (x/1, $40000), then three locked `???` at $200k, $450k, $1.4m.
- Other known items: trampolines, conveyors, panels, piggy bank (catches dropped coins so gnomes cannot grab them), magnet drop worth millions.
- Scenery: backyard with house, garden umbrella, BBQ, flamingos, armchair on the lawn, pine forest, mushrooms, birdhouse, rocky cliff, neighbourhood to expand into. Painterly low-poly look.
- Picking up certain items triggers "secret events".

Sources: Steam store page, Steam community hub (patch notes 30 Sep), itch.io page, incrementaldb.com, 6 screenshots.
