## Findings
- F1 `src/main.ts:108-111` + `src/ui/menu.ts:41,132`: when the player clicks RESUME in the pause menu and the browser refuses the lock, the menu stays and the game stays paused, because the button calls only `ctx.input.lock()` and `onLockError` (`main.ts:166-168`) ignores errors while `menu.visible`. Esc in the same state resumes with a free cursor. (CONFIRMED by review-debugger: 10/10 frames menu visible, paused)
- F2 `src/ui/style.css:278`: when the player owns 2 or more tools, the free-cursor pill (`bottom: 110px`, ~44 px tall) covers the gear line `.hud-gear` (`style.css:84`, `bottom: 112px`) and touches the first-use hint `.hud-hint` (`style.css:149`, `bottom: 150px`). At 640 px wide (narrow media) the centred ~380 px pill also reaches into `.hud-left` (`style.css:70`, width 210, down to `bottom: 94px`). (confirmed)
- F3 `src/core/input.ts:43-46`: when the cursor is free and the pointer leaves the window on one side and comes back on the other, the first `mousemove` carries the whole jump in `movementX` (632 px in a 640 px window, ~80 degrees of yaw; ~240 degrees at 1920 px), because Blink keeps the last in-page position. (CONFIRMED by review-debugger, measured in Chromium)
- F4 `src/main.ts:183-185` + `src/core/input.ts:26-27`: when the cursor is free, only a left `click` captures it; right button does nothing (`contextmenu` prevented, `mousedown` ignored without the lock), so a player who right-clicks to throw gets no response. (confirmed)

- G3 = F3. Class: a free-cursor `movementX/Y` delta used without dropping the jump after the pointer left the page. Class grep `movementX|movementY` in `src`: 1 hit, `input.ts:44-45`, in group.
