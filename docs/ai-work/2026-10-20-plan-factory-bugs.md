### Assumptions (numbered)
1. The yaw steps in the clip come from pointer-lock mouse events (cursor warp / OS acceleration on macOS Chromium), not from game code: `yaw` has a single writer (`player.ts:399`) and nothing else in the frame touches it; the camera shake adds at most 0.05 rad (`fx.shake` cap). Inference from the diagnosis, not measured; the chute collider removes the second, visual half of the symptom (the view inside the chute).
2. Dropping one raw report > 250 px never eats a real move: a raw report covers 1-8 ms of mouse travel; 250 px in 8 ms is 31 px/ms = 0.5 m/s at 1600 dpi, above a hand flick at that polling rate. At low fps the frame's `mousemove` sums many reports, which is why the filter runs on `getCoalescedEvents()` and not on the frame event.
3. A chute collider 0.2 m shorter than the mesh keeps the mouth free for the smallest tier (0.37 m margin) and the near plane cannot reach the uncovered 0.2 m (capsule radius 0.35).
4. Gnomes standing at the new wall until the next wander pick (a few seconds) is acceptable: it is what the fence and the house do today; PP asked for the hitbox, not for steering.
5. Robots: the stuck fallback stays for the desk, house and props (robots-avoid decision, same day).
6. `scenario-robots-avoid.js` and `scenario-robots.js` live in other sessions' scratchpads; if absent, the regression is the perf number in row 8 (`machines.update` p95 stays <= 0.2 ms with 4 robots, measured as in robots-avoid `perf`).

### Alternatives rejected
- (a) One big factory collider (body + chute + pedestal in one box): rejected, the player could not reach the lever post from the front and the chute's underside would be an invisible wall 0.8 m in front of the chute for thrown gnomes.
- (b) Gnome keep-away push from the factory like the hole push (`gnomes.ts:582`): rejected for now (rung 1, not ordered); listed as open decision 3.
- (c) Mouse: per-frame cap of 250 px on the `mousemove` event: rejected, at 15 fps (the clip's frame rate near the factory) a normal 180° flick is 476 px per frame and would be eaten.
- (d) Mouse: `unadjustedMovement` only, no filter: kept as open decision 1 option a; rejected as the default because the brief names both and the cause is inferred, not measured.
- (e) Robots: let the new colliders plus the stuck fallback handle the factory: rejected, every robot with a post near the factory would bump, wait 1.5 s and sidestep on every trip (robots-avoid measured 10 s stalls with that fallback).

### Chosen variant
(A) Solid factory + raw input + measurement. The chute, pedestal+pipe and gear get static colliders the size of their meshes on the existing factory body; the spawn point rises 0.4 m so every tier starts above the lip with the same landing. Pros: the visible half of the bug (view inside the chute, gnomes and trucks under it) is fixed by geometry; the mouse half gets the one thing PP's hardware can tell us tomorrow; no path can make a real flick worse. Cons: if the jump is a cursor warp that survives raw input, tonight's build still jumps once in a while, and PP reads it in the log instead of feeling it fixed.
