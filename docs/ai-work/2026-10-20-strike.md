# STRIKE: bowling with gnomes

Plan: `docs/ai-work/2026-10-20-plan-night-features.md`, section 2.3 "P2 STRIKE" (card B in 2.2, review focus 2.4).

## Problem

A gnome thrown hard by the player that knocks 2 or more other gnomes into the hole should feel like a bowling strike: big text, money bonus, fanfare, gold sparks. 5 or more is a TURKEY.

## Summary

New file `src/systems/strike.ts`, class `Strike` with `update(dt)`. No other src file touched.

- Each frame, every gnome with `thrownBy === 'player'`, `state === 'ragdoll'` and speed > 6 m/s marks the free gnomes within 1.6 m (`forEachNear`; hits are collected in the callback, marks are written after it). Other fast player throws are not marked.
- A marked gnome keeps its last position, read only while it is still in `ctx.gnomes.list`. When it leaves the list within 2 s of its mark and its last position was within `holeRadius + 0.5` of the hole centre, it counts for the gnome that hit it.
- A chain pays when no new gnome joined it for 0.5 s, or 2 s after its first count. With 2+ gnomes: `hole.earn(25 x count x valueScale)`, `hud.bigText('STRIKE xN', '#ffd23f', '+$...')`, `audio.fanfare(3)`, one gold `fx.burst` at the hole (8 + 4 per gnome, max 30 sparks). 5+ shows `TURKEY! STRIKE xN` at a larger size.
- Own clock (sum of `dt`), so a pause (`update` not called, or called with 0) does not expire anything. No saved state: a reload starts empty, an old save needs nothing.

Decisions taken here:
- 0.5 s settle delay: without it the player would see "STRIKE x2" and a moment later "STRIKE x3" for one throw. The plan does not say when a chain pays; the settle keeps one text per throw and stays inside the 2 s window.
- `TURKEY! STRIKE x5` instead of only `TURKEY`: the plan's check looks for "STRIKE" in `.hud-big` and a 5-pin strike must pass it.
- `Map` instead of `WeakMap` for the marks: the system has to walk the marked gnomes every frame to cache their positions, and a `WeakMap` cannot be iterated. Entries are dropped 2 s after the mark or when the gnome leaves the list, so nothing leaks.
- Drabina: rung 2, everything reuses existing APIs (`forEachNear`, `hole.earn`, `hole.valueScale`, `hud.bigText`, `audio.fanfare`, `fx.burst`, `money()`). No new sound: the plan's card mentions a "pin-crash sound" but the P2 spec says `audio.fanfare(3)` and `audio.ts` is not my file.

Rejected:
- Pin-crash sound: needs a new method in `src/core/audio.ts`, which this package may not edit; the spec line uses `fanfare(3)`.

```

ctx.strike = new Strike();
      ctx.strike.update(dt);
```
