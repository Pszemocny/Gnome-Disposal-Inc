### Assumptions (numbered)
1. The hitches on PP's laptop are a mix of GPU load (pixels, shadow pass, MSAA) and one-time CPU stalls (first-sight shader links, texture uploads); the clip's frozen frames near the factory/house are consistent with first-sight compiles of materials the title camera never saw. Inference from code (`rg compile src` → 0), not measured on PP's machine; rows 9 and 21 and the factory agent's `slow-frame` notes are the proof paths.
2. The Mac reproduces CPU-side spikes (physics, GC, compiles, setItem) at the same order of magnitude as a laptop; it does not reproduce GPU-bound frame times, hence the proxy regime of row 16 (33 MP backing store forces the GPU to be the bottleneck even on Apple silicon) and the pixel table instead of "fast".
3. High = today's Medium values (OD 2 a). PP compares "before" and "after" by switching to High.
4. `import('/src/core/settings.ts')` from the page returns the live module instance on a freshly started dev server (the quality note hit a second instance only after HMR); step 1 asserts it.
5. `renderer.compileAsync` with every object already in the scene (machines restored) covers all programs the player can meet without buying anything; materials created later (new tool types, mushrooms, UFO, tornado, hole rays) still link on first sight once each; rows 11-14 catch them if they matter (programsDelta > 0).
6. The shadow box ±25 m on Medium leaves shadows within 25 m of the player; beyond that the ground is lit. With the texel snap in `environment.ts:434-445` the edge of that area moves in whole texels, as today.
7. `prefs.quality === 'low'` stays the only reader that changes the night look; Medium keeps the night.

### Alternatives rejected
- (a) MSAA off per quality: `antialias` is a renderer constructor option, so it needs a reload and a "takes effect after reload" note the quality note rejected; at ratio 1 the edges need MSAA most. Rejected.
- (b) Shadow map every other frame (`shadowMap.autoUpdate = false`): the sun follows the player each frame, so the shadow area would jump at 30 Hz. Rejected.
- (c) Fewer tufts/mushrooms on Medium: 146 k of 370 k static tri in two instanced draw calls; the main pass is fill-bound (4.67 MP), not vertex-bound. Rejected, numbers in row 23.
- (d) Removing the two point lights (lamp, hole glow): saves two light evaluations per fragment; the hole glow is part of the look at the one place PP watches most; only if rows 11-16 still fail after 5d, as a separate order. Rejected here.
- (e) Sync `renderer.compile`: blocks boot for all programs at once (hundreds of ms on a laptop) before the title shows. `compileAsync` instead.
- (f) Per-system attribution in the `?stats` overlay: 15 timing lines inside the game loop that three other plans edit; the headless scenario has the attribution, the overlay needs only three numbers from PP's machine.
- (g) Physics catch-up cap 3 → 2 steps: slows game time under load; nothing measured says physics is the top system. Not in the plan; row 14 prints its p95.
- (h) Keep 2048²/ratio 2 as High: see OD 2.
- (i) Relying only on the factory agent's `slow-frame` notes instead of `?stats`: they fire only near the factory and only above 100 ms, so a steady 25-40 ms stutter at the hole would leave no trace. Kept as a second source, not the only one.
