# Changelog

Newest build first. Each build lists what changed for the player. Shown in the start menu (button "What's new").

## 2026-10-28 10:00

- The second level of Midas Spores costs more, $6m instead of $5m; the other levels keep their prices.
- Spore Storm and Midas Spores are weaker, so Gold Midas no longer runs almost nonstop with every upgrade bought.
- Gnomes turned gold by the Golden Hour now pay a tenth of a real golden gnome and give half its XP.
- Gnomes fed by machines, robots and kicks drop mushrooms less often.
- Robots leave the King alone, and fans push him much less: he is heavy.
- Feeding Frenzy costs more: its last level is now $1m.
- The leaderboard is wider, so all four tabs fit on one line, each in a gnome hat colour.

## 2026-10-27 18:00

- The online version now sends anonymous progress statistics: hole levels, money, purchases and achievements. No names unless you enter one on the leaderboard.

## 2026-10-27 14:00

- Right-click Strong Arm in the shop to take it one level down. Every change, up or down, costs like the next level, so switching back and forth gets dearer.
- New achievement No Scope: score a SNIPER shot before you buy the Trick Scope.
- Each time the yard grows, the hole slides over to the other side of the yard. Robots in its way step aside.
- New leaderboard tab BIGGEST COMBO: your best combo of the run is sent with your score when you jump into the hole.

## 2026-10-27 10:00

- Press N on a placed fan to turn its power down to 75, 50 or 25 %.
- New tool: the Gnome Funnel. Hang it in the air; gnomes that fall into it shoot out towards the hole.
- A gnome you throw or drop into the funnel yourself gives full XP. The funnel shot itself earns no trick bonuses.
- Lands are no longer bought: the yard grows by itself at hole levels 3, 5 and 8, always toward the hole. Lands you already paid for are refunded.
- The GNOME letters moved to new spots in the growing yard.

## 2026-10-26 18:00

- The golden shovel and the GNOME letters stay gold but no longer glow.
- Trick shot bonuses start small and reach full size at hole level 4, so the golden hoop no longer buys the whole early shop.

## 2026-10-26 13:00

- Every Fuses upgrade now starts at $1,000. Levels you already bought stay.
- Golden Hour is now a mushroom instead of a random event: eat it and every gnome turns to gold for 25 s.
- The Golden Hour, Gold Midas and Rainbow mushrooms drop only from hole level 6, so the start stays calm.
- Combo Cap has six more levels for big money, so the combo can climb higher.
- New late upgrades from hole level 8: Bottomless Hunger (more XP), Spore Storm (more mushrooms) and Midas Spores (almost only Gold Midas). They cost big money.
- The King now gives 1000 XP, and shooting down a UFO fills a big part of the XP bar.
- Shadow Gnomes step out of the dark behind you, stare and make a strange quiet clicking. Robots and tools leave them alone for a while.
- Every cleaning robot now has a station with its fuse box and a lamp that turns red when it needs E. Saboteurs must walk to the station, and E, G and X work on the station as well as on the robot.

## 2026-10-26 12:00

- Every big combo text (Gnomazing! and up) makes the hole spit out a mushroom.

## 2026-10-26 10:00

- Coin Magnet moved to the Magnet tab, next to the other magnets. Your bought levels stay.
- The last level of Strong Arm makes weight irrelevant: every gnome flies the same, from your hands or from the Magnet Gun.

## 2026-10-25 22:00

- Eclipse Cap changes the sky slower: 5 s of day, a 5 s fade to night, 5 s of night and a 5 s fade back.
- The lesson that asked for a trick shot off a trampoline is gone. The TRICK x3 bonus still pays when you pull it off.

## 2026-10-25 20:00

- The secret code is gone: five gold letters G N O M E now lie around the lands, the far ones visible beyond the fence. Feed them all to the hole for BOOZED mode, once per save.
- The golden shovel now glints, so a sharp eye can spot it.

## 2026-10-25 16:00

- Dumping a crate into the hole now pays full hole XP for every gnome in it. A crate tipped over at the rim still pays a quarter.
- A Magnet Gun ball flies as one body at the speed of its heaviest gnome, so the whole ball lands in one clump.
- Golden gnomes no longer drop extra mushrooms: every gnome has the same chance, only the wizard drops more.
- New Factory upgrade Feeding Frenzy (hole level 4, 5 levels): more Massive gnomes per level, all of them at level 5. While you own it, gnomes give normal XP and fly as far as light ones.
- Golden gnomes shine like metal, and Gold Midas turns every gnome in the yard gold while it lasts. Hats keep their colour.
- New mushroom Eclipse Cap: x2 money for 20 s while the sky slowly swings between day and night.
- The Piggy Bank is gone: Coin Magnet now has 10 levels, and level 10 sends coins to your bank by itself. Old saves get the $800 back.
- The shop trees are tidy rows: each tool reads left to right, the tool, its upgrades, then its Fuses. Rows go from the cheapest unlocked tool down to the locked ones, and no line crosses another row.

## 2026-10-25 12:00

- A moved robot's area ring shows again: its post now always lands on the ground, even when you place it aiming far ahead.
- The leaderboard asks for one name or nickname, up to 20 characters; digits and . _ - ' are fine, a full first and last name is no longer asked for.
- You sign the leaderboard only after jumping into the hole (and at the end of a daily); Leaderboard in the Esc menu just shows the boards.
- New tab FASTEST LVL 12: your unpaused time to hole level 12, sent with every rebirth. The money tab is now called MOST MONEY and counts money earned in the run, spent or not.

## 2026-10-24 18:00

- You can now own up to 20 Cooling Fans, and they are cheaper per fan than before. Big Blades, a new fan upgrade, makes every fan blow harder.
- Wide Belt makes every conveyor belt wider, so gnomes ride side by side. Placed belts widen at once; a belt with no room goes back to your inventory.
- Every tool now has its own Fuses upgrade in the Tools tree. An old save gives every tool the Fuses level you already had.
- The highest upgrade levels cost more, so the shop lasts to the end of a run; tools, crates and the Deep keep their prices. A maxed save keeps its levels, the new prices apply from your next rebirth.
- The Deep has more levels to spend Hole Shards on.
- Esc in the shop, blueprints or legend now always takes you back into the game, never to the pause menu. The "Click or press W to capture the mouse" note is gone.
- A new game opens with a short intro about the company, the factory and the hole. Click or press a key to move on, or just wait.
- A mushroom never stays inside the hole any more: it hops out to just past the rim.
- The start screen now tells the story in two sentences; all controls are under H.

## 2026-10-21 12:00

- The UFO and the King come more often. The King always arrives once, 6 s after the hole reaches level 10.
- Nothing slows the game down any more: big combos and an eaten King keep their text, shake and sound at full speed. Only the Tab tool wheel still slows the game while you hold it.
- Hole XP now depends on how you feed it: full for a hand throw, less per gnome for a Magnet Gun ball, a quarter for machines, crates, robots, the plow, the blower or a kick. Money stays the same, and the King always gives his full 200 XP.
- A crate tipped over next to the hole counts like a crate dump, without trick shots.
- Up to 10 Cooling Fans and 10 Conveyor Belts (was 4 and 6), and the extra ones cost more. Every placed tool keeps its own fuses.

## 2026-10-21 08:00

- Errors from the online version are reported to the developers, without names or addresses. The game keeps running when a report cannot be sent.
- Smoother play near the factory and the hole: the default Medium graphics are lighter, with shadows that end closer to you. High is the old Medium look, and your yard is prepared on the title screen.

## 2026-10-21 02:00

- One shared online leaderboard for everyone on the web version, with ALL-TIME and today's DAILY. Your score is always saved on your computer first, and you see your local scores when the online board is out of reach.
- Names are shown publicly online, so the name field no longer suggests your real name.
- Goals teach the game: the tutorial ends with a 5 combo, and after "Collect coins" a LESSON line shows one mechanic at a time (mushroom, gnome types, heavy gnomes, the crate, combo 15, trick shots). Lessons you can do count together, and old saves are never paid twice.
- During the mushroom lesson the next gnome you feed is sure to drop a mushroom, never inside a placed tool.
- The factory is solid: you, gnomes and robots no longer walk into the chute, the pedestal or the big gear, and robots drive around it. A burst, a parade or the King comes out one gnome after another, and a gnome that bounces back onto the chute stays there until you grab it.
- A tool from an old save that stood inside the factory goes back to your inventory.
- With the mouse captured, the game uses raw mouse movement without system acceleration, so the sensitivity slider may feel different in Chrome. View jumps and slow frames near the factory are logged for the developers.

## 2026-10-21 00:30

- Shadows on the grass no longer crawl and shimmer while you walk.

## 2026-10-20 22:50

- Esc takes you straight back into the game, from the pause menu and from the shop, blueprints and legend; RESUME works the same. No more "Click to play" screen.
- When the browser keeps the mouse, the game still runs with a small "Click or press W" note, and the first click only captures the mouse. The Tab wheel still works, and switching to another window pauses the game.

## 2026-10-20 21:46

- The game is now called Gnome Disposal Inc., with a new logo and tab title. Your save, blueprints and leaderboard stay.

## 2026-10-20 21:38

- Offline earnings are gone: no money for the time the game was closed. Machines and the factory only work while the game is open.

## 2026-10-20 19:00

- Trick shots no longer slow down time; a BULLSEYE keeps its text, bonus, sparks, fanfare and shake. A 60 or 100 combo and an eaten King still play a short slow-mo.

## 2026-10-20 18:30

- Garden Gnomes waddle and swing longer arms, so you can see them walk from behind. Other gnome types sway the same way and have the new arms.

## 2026-10-20 17:00

- The magnet panel no longer covers hotbar slot 1, and hotbar slots shrink when room is short. On a narrow window the hotbar moves right of the magnet panel and still fits.

## 2026-10-20 16:55

- Garden Gnomes are built from moving parts: legs and arms swing with every step, and the hat and beard lag behind on turns. Other gnome types, and gnomes in the instant replay, still move as one piece.
- Fans, wheels, vacuums, black holes, belts, the factory gear, mushrooms and the UFO now spin up and down smoothly. The push itself still starts and stops at once.
- The shop is a big dark computer screen with taskbar tabs, bigger pictures and a short power-on flash. "AVAILABLE: n" shows how many things you can buy now, and the bottom bar repeats the last buy message.
- One column on the left holds GOAL, CONTRACT, a new EFFECTS card and the messages, with nothing on top of anything. At most 4 messages show, and a repeated one shows "×2".
- "Crate full!" now floats above the crate itself.
- Buying in the shop no longer adds a message, and the H controls card moved to the right under the money.

## 2026-10-20 15:55

- Gnomes at the far end of a fan's stream no longer get knocked over again each time they stand up.
- The Magnet Gun is a hand tool next to Hands, the Plow Tractor and the Leaf Blower: hold Tab to pick one, and C switches between it and bare hands. Placeable tools stay on 1-0, and the line above the hotbar shows your tool and what C gives.
- Buying the Magnet Gun puts it in your hand, and every tool switch drops what you hold.
- With Trick Scope Lvl 2 or higher, held gnomes say nothing, so their speech no longer covers the glass.
- Cleaning robots ignore gnomes in the air and no longer drive in circles under them. A flying gnome becomes a target again once it lands.
- Trick Scope has 3 levels, and old saves keep Lvl 1. Lvl 1 colours the crosshair by where the throw lands, Lvl 2 names the trick in a round glass, Lvl 3 adds the distance and the multiplier.
- P saves a screenshot of the whole screen with the HUD, shop and menus (test builds only). Messages now show above the shop and menus.

## 2026-10-20 15:40

- A green number on each shop tab shows how many things you can buy there right now.
- The Magnet Gun beam is gone: hold LMB and every gnome in the cone flies into the ball at once. The ball takes as many as it fits, the rest waits on the ground.
- A Magnet Gun ball with more than 3 gnomes stays quiet, so no speech bubbles cover the crosshair.

## 2026-10-20 15:10

- Fuses (Tools tab, from hole level 5): a saboteur hit turns one fuse red, and a tool breaks only when none is left. E on a broken tool fixes it and swaps one red fuse for a green one, each further E one more.
- A lever next to the factory switches Auto Crank on and off with E. OFF stops only automatic production; holding E and events still make gnomes.
- The Magnet Gun volley text names its trick shots, and the volley cap rose from x4 to x8. So AIR adds on top of LONG SHOT or SNIPER.

## 2026-10-20 13:40

- The shop is a skill tree on every tab, Land and Deep too, with columns by hole level and lines to the upgrade each one needs. Tools show a picture of their 3D model, and colours show what you can afford, own or have maxed.
- The first shop open in a session draws the tool pictures, which can take a moment.

## 2026-10-20 12:45

- A "What's new" button in the start menu and the pause menu shows this list.

## 2026-10-20 12:20

- Crash fixed: the game no longer freezes when a robot chases a gnome that vanished.
- Errors are saved on the computer serving the game, and a box with a Copy button shows in the bottom left.
- E repairs the tool under the crosshair also while you hold a tool to place.
- Up to 12 cleaning robots (was 4), and they are cheaper.
- Robots pick dangerous gnomes first, then the gnome lying still longest, then the nearest. Two robots never chase the same gnome.

## 2026-10-20 11:40

- Robots drive around placed tools and no longer get stuck between trampolines. They back off when stuck, give up gnomes they cannot reach and return to their post after 15 s.
